About the job
AI Engineer - Job Description

Key Responsibility

Adopt an AI-first approach in designing and delivering solutions, leveraging AI/LLMs to optimize development, decision-making, and productivity.
Design, develop, and deploy scalable AI/ML models to solve real-world problems.
Build LLM-powered applications such as chatbots, copilots, and AI agents.
Design and implement agent-based systems capable of reasoning, tool usage, and multi-step task execution.
Develop and optimize RAG pipelines (embedding, retrieval, re-ranking) to ensure high accuracy and performance.
Evaluate and select appropriate models and architectures (LLMs, classification, regression, clustering, recommendation, etc.).
Fine-tune and optimize models for accuracy, latency, and cost efficiency.
Leverage AI tools (e.g., GPT, Copilot, Claude) to automate workflows and enhance engineering productivity.
Build and maintain LLM orchestration pipelines using frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph or similar.
Monitor, evaluate, and improve model performance using tools such as LangSmith or equivalent.
Collaborate with product, backend, and infrastructure teams to deliver end-to-end AI solutions.
Take ownership of assigned modules or projects and deliver results independently.
Continuously explore and integrate cutting-edge AI technologies into products and internal workflows.
Deliver measurable impact through AI solutions (e.g., improved accuracy, reduced cost, increased automation rate).
Lead or co-lead AI/ML initiatives, ensuring technical direction, quality, and timely delivery.
Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers or team members.
Participate in solution architecture design and make key technical decisions for AI systems.
Collaborate with stakeholders to define AI solution roadmap and translate business needs into technical implementations.
Review code, models, and system design to ensure best practices and scalability.

Qualification Requirements

Technical Skills

Strong AI-first mindset: proactively leveraging AI tools to improve efficiency, automate tasks, and support decision-making.
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or related field (or equivalent experience).
5+ years of experience as an AI Engineer or similar role.
Proficient in Python and familiar with ML/AI frameworks.
Hands-on experience building and deploying AI/ML models in production.
Strong ability to work independently: from research and prototyping to implementation and testing.
Experience working with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure).
Experience in leading or mentoring a small team (or owning end-to-end AI modules/projects).

Priorities (not a must-have)

Experience with RAG and vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS).
Experience building real-world AI products (chatbots, virtual assistants, smart search systems).
Experience designing APIs or integrating AI into applications.
Strong prompt engineering and workflow orchestration skills.
Ability to read research papers and rapidly prototype new ideas.
Experience as a technical lead or owning AI solutions in production environments.
Experience working in cross-functional teams and driving technical alignment.

Personal Skills

Good communication skills in Vietnamese and English (written and spoken).
Self-driven, proactive, and high sense of responsibility.
Strong analytical thinking and attention to detail.
Team player with a collaborative mindset.
